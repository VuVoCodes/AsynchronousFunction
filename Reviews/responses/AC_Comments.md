# Author-AC confidential comment

We sincerely thank the AC for the careful meta-review, and especially for the concluding assessment that the probe-guided monitoring idea is original, simple, and suitable for a Concept and Feasibility contribution: exactly what we hoped to communicate. We answer the six points compactly (full detail in the public threads), candidly, including where our own numbers moved downward.

**[1] CREMA-D discrepancy.** 61.59 is our joint-training baseline, not OGM-GE's number. Peng et al. (CVPR 2022) report **61.9** for OGM-GE on CREMA-D (their Table 1, CREMA-D Acc column, Concatenation$\dagger$ row; again their Table 2, OGM-GE row; their baseline is 51.7; 1-frame protocol per their Sec. 4.2). Our 1-frame ablation reproduces this at $62.47 \pm 1.42$; our main table uses the richer 3-frame protocol. We will relabel the rows to prevent conflation (miLe Q7).

**[2] Adam.** Per-step instrumentation confirms miLe's mechanism, stated plainly in that thread: under SGD the boost transmits essentially one-to-one to parameter updates (1.64 applied scale, 1.50 update-norm ratio), while Adam absorbs roughly two-thirds (1.48 applied, 1.17 update). The accuracy consequence, tested with the composed contrast at full budget under both optimizers on the identical CMU-MOSI pipeline (5 matched seeds): **+1.69 pp under SGD** ($p=0.032$, 5/5 seed pairs) and $-0.09$ pp under Adam ($p=0.90$). SGD is not retuned there, so the contrast is within-optimizer only (miLe W3).

**[3] Isolating PGGB from OGM-GE.** We tripled the seed count (final five fixed in advance under a stopping rule, all 15 reported): $\alpha=0$ arm $69.40 \pm 1.25$ vs PGGB+OGM-GE $71.00 \pm 1.46$: **+1.60 pp, 95% CI [0.59, 2.62]**, Welch $p=0.0032$, $d=1.18$, paired t $p=0.016$. We state plainly that **this revises the headline downward**: +2.31 (original 5) to +2.06 ($n=10$) to +1.60 ($n=15$); the original seeds were favorable draws. The later batches are not individually significant (~38% power each); the sample was sequentially extended with all runs reported, and the pooled endpoint (88% power), committed before the final batch, excludes zero. Table 2's 0.22 pp is the 1-frame setting, where starved visual input compresses every method's margin; the 3-frame decomposition becomes the primary ablation (miLe W4). On breadth: near-neutrality on the four low-imbalance benchmarks is intended behavior, and prior methods regress there (OGM-GE: KS $-1.80$, Twitter15 $-0.27$, Sarcasm $-0.59$; miLe W2).

**[4] Trajectories and warm-up (tQk1).** All 500 probe events instrumented: the first event is at chance (raw 21%/28% against 16.67%), identification is correct from event 2 (iteration 39), and the ordering inverts only twice more, both where the near-zero gap's sign is estimator noise. Worst-case misdirection exposure is one $K$-step window at scale $\le 1.23$. No warm-up is needed. Measured overhead +1.61% for probes (+7.34% composed, largely OGM-GE's own), memory unchanged.

**[5] Imbalance score and threshold (gN93).** $\delta$ is the final-epoch EMA-smoothed probe gap during baseline training, 5-seed mean (App. B.9). Not tuned: the values split into a low group (0.017-0.150) and a high group (0.178-0.268), every threshold in $[0.150, 0.178)$ yields the identical categorization, and $\delta$ is measured before any method runs. Twitter15 and CMU-MOSEI are the seed-sensitive boundary cases and will be flagged, with the definition moved into Sec. 4.1 (gN93 Q2).

**[6] Theory scoping.** We agree the propositions are safety properties, not gap-closure or rate results; the abstract and introduction will be aligned ("we establish three safety properties"). Details in tQk1 (W3) and gN93 (Q1).
